About the studio

Kuhak (কুহক) means illusion, enchantment: the spell a good story casts. Kuhak Studio is an independent game studio from Bangladesh, making games from the culture, history, traditions and legends of Bengal.

Our worlds come from the places and stories we grew up with: village paths and paddy fields, rivers, khals and the monsoon, crowded city lanes, festivals, crafts and food, the legends of kings, rebels and saints, and the ghost tales told after dark. Each game takes one piece of that heritage and lets you live it.

We make them for players everywhere, whether you grew up in Bengal or have never been there, so these stories travel further than ever.

What we make

Bengal’s heritage, made playable

01

Rooted in heritage

History, legends, festivals, crafts, music, food and folklore: the living culture of Bengal is our source material, researched and treated with respect.

02

The setting is the game

Paddy fields and river crossings, the monsoon and the Kalboishakhi, old city lanes: places and seasons that shape how you play, not just how it looks.

03

Bengal’s own voice

Eerie, funny or epic, our games speak the way Bengal speaks: regional dialects, proverbs and the humour you hear in every tea stall.

04

For everyone

Made in Bangladesh for players everywhere: in Bangla and English, easy to pick up, and built to run on budget phones.
