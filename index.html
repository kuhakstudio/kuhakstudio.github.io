About the studio

Kuhak (কুহক) means illusion, enchantment: the spell a story casts on a dark night. Kuhak Studio is an independent game studio from Bangladesh, making games out of the folk tales, villages and nights of Bengal.

Our worlds are built from the places we grew up with: mud roads and paddy fields, khals and bamboo groves, the cremation ground at the edge of the village, the old tree nobody walks under after dark. And they are full of the ghosts our grandparents told us about.

We make them for players everywhere, whether or not you have ever heard of a Shakchunni.

What we make

Games where the setting is the game

01

Folklore first

Shakchunni, Petni, Mechho Bhoot: the ghosts of Bengali folk tales, with the habits and rules people still whisper about. Each one plays the way its story goes.

02

Bengal as a world

Mud roads, broken bamboo bridges, flooded paddy, a khal crossed on a banana-trunk raft, a Kalboishakhi storm. Not scenery: they change how you play.

03

Eerie, and funny

Our horror knows how to laugh at itself. Ghosts speak in village dialects, a scarecrow hops after you, and sometimes a broom is all that stands between you and a ghost bride.
