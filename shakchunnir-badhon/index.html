Shakchunnir Badhon (শাঁকচুন্নির বাঁধন)

A comic folk-horror endless runner

Run from Shakchunni through a haunted Bengal village. Dodge the ghosts of our folk tales, keep your lantern lit, and don't let her catch you.

  • Android
  • Free, with ads
  • Bangla & English
  • For ages 13+
Coming soon to Google Play

The story

Tonight, she wants a husband

Kalu is a young man walking home late, his lantern swinging, humming to keep the dark away. Then she comes out of the trees: Shakchunni, the ghost of Bengali folk tales who wears a married woman's white shakha bangles and has never stopped wanting to be a wife.

She has picked her groom. Kalu has other plans. He runs: down the broken mud road, through bamboo groves and the cremation ground, along the narrow aal between flooded paddy fields, over rickety bamboo sankos and across the khal on a banana-trunk raft, while every ghost of the village gets in his way.

She giggles. She calls him jamai. She will not stop.

How it plays

Simple to play, hard to escape

⇆

Run

Swipe to change lanes and take the corners, swipe up to jump, down to slide. The further you go, the faster it gets.

✦

Keep the light

Your lantern burns down. Grab oil bottles, find a kupi. When it goes out, the ghosts close in and she catches you in the dark.

Kori & tabij

Collect kori, the old cowrie-shell money, and spend it on a tabij: for a few seconds no ghost can touch you.

The jharu

Caught? With a jharu in your bag, whack her across the face, watch her fly, and run on.

Meet the ghosts

Straight out of grandmother’s ghost stories

Every ghost on the road comes from the stories told in Bengali villages after dark, and each one plays the way its story goes.

শাঁকচুন্নি

Shakchunni

The ghost of a married woman, known by her white conch-shell bangles. She longs to be a wife again.

In the game: she chases you all night. Stumble twice and she has you.

মেছো ভূত

Mechho Bhoot

The fish-loving ghost of ponds and marshes, said to follow fishermen home for their catch.

In the game: bursts out of the mud (jump it), or out of the khal (steer round it).

পেত্নী

Petni

A restless female ghost who makes her home in old trees and dangles from the branches.

In the game: drops down in front of you. Slide under her.

পালকির বেহারা

The palki bearers

A ghostly wedding procession that crosses the road at midnight, chanting.

In the game: they carry the palki across both lanes. Slide under the pole.

গেছো ভূত

Gechho Bhoot

The ghost that lives inside a tree and doesn't like being disturbed.

In the game: throws its whole tree across the road. Jump it or slide under.

কাকতাড়ুয়া

Kaktarua

The scarecrow of the paddy fields, with a clay-pot head. Tonight, it's awake.

In the game: hops into your lane on the aal. Switch lanes.

Questions

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes. The game is free and shows ads. Ads that give you a reward (to keep running after you are caught, or towards a jharu) are always your choice.

When and where can I play it?

It is coming soon to Google Play for Android phones.

Which languages?

Bangla and English, switchable in the settings. The ghosts speak in a village dialect, with subtitles.

Is it very scary?

It is comic horror: spooky, dark and full of ghosts, but played for laughs. No gore. It is meant for players aged 13 and up.

Will it run on my phone?

It is built for budget Android phones too. The graphics setting picks the right level automatically, and you can change it yourself.

What data does the game collect?

The game keeps your progress on your phone only. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which collects some device data. See the privacy policy.
