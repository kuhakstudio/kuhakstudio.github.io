This policy explains what information the Android game Shakchunnir Badhon ("the game") handles. The game is made by Kuhak Studio ("we", "us"). We do not run any servers for the game and we do not collect your personal information ourselves.

1. What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves only game data on your own device: your settings (language, sound, graphics), your best distance, your kori, jharu and progress. This data never leaves your phone and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID / AD_ID),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps), and crash or performance diagnostics.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's consent form), and you can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads), which also limits ad personalisation. If you choose not to share it, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

Rewarded ads (watching an ad to keep running, or towards a jharu) are always your choice.

3. Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google Play's handling of your data (downloads, purchases, crash reports you choose to share) is covered by Google's privacy policy.

4. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Security

Information sent by the Google advertising service travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We have no copy of it.

6. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, requests about data collected for advertising are handled by Google (see the links above). You can always contact us with questions.

7. Changes

If the game changes what it collects (for example, if we add a leaderboard), we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.
