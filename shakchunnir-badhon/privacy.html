Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026 · Kuhak Studio

Englishবাংলা

English

This policy explains what information the Android game Shakchunnir Badhon ("the game") handles. The game is made by Kuhak Studio ("we", "us"). We do not run any servers for the game and we do not collect your personal information ourselves.

1. What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves only game data on your own device: your settings (language, sound, graphics), your best distance, your kori, jharu and progress. None of it is sent to us. Android's own backup (if you have it turned on) may include this game data in your Google account's device backup, so it can come back when you reinstall the game.

2. Google Play Games (sign-in and cloud save)

The game can use Google Play Games services, a service of Google LLC. If you are signed in to Google Play Games, or you tap Sign in: Google Play Games in Settings, the game receives your Play Games player profile (player ID, gamer name and avatar) from Google. It uses it only to keep your progress in your own Google account.

Cloud save. While you are signed in, the game stores one saved game in your Google account through Google Play Games' Saved Games: your best distance and totals, your kori, jharu and owned items, and when it was saved. It is used only to bring your progress back on a new phone or after reinstalling. It is stored by Google, not by us, and we have no copy of it. Settings and ad data are not included.

Signing in is optional; the game works fully without it. You can delete the saved game and your Play Games data for this game at any time from your Play Games profile (play.google.com/games/profile) or your Google Account. Google handles this data under its privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android advertising ID / AD_ID),
  • your IP address, from which an approximate location can be derived,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • how you interact with ads (views, taps), and crash or performance diagnostics.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. How Google uses information from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown (Google's consent form), and you can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings → Ad privacy choices. On any Android phone you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads), which also limits ad personalisation. If you choose not to share it, you still see ads, but they are not personalised.

Rewarded ads (watching an ad to keep running, or towards a jharu) are always your choice.

4. Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google Play's handling of your data (downloads, purchases, crash reports you choose to share) is covered by Google's privacy policy.

5. Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Security

Information sent to Google's advertising and Play Games services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). We have no copy of it. The game's own data is kept in the game's private storage on your phone, which other apps cannot read.

7. Keeping and deleting data

We keep no data about you, so we have nothing to store or delete. The game's own data stays on your phone until you uninstall the game or clear its storage (on most phones: Settings → Apps → Shakchunnir Badhon → Storage → Clear storage). The cloud save in your Google account stays until you delete it from your Play Games profile. Google keeps the advertising data it collects for the periods described in its policy: policies.google.com/technologies/retention.

8. Your rights

Because we do not hold your personal data, requests about data collected for advertising or kept by Google Play Games are handled by Google (see the links above). You can always contact us with questions.

9. Changes

If the game changes what it collects (for example, if we add a leaderboard), we will update this page and its effective date before the change is released.

10. Contact

Kuhak Studio · support@kuhakstudio.com

ক্লাউডে সেভ। সাইন ইন থাকলে গেম Google Play Games-এর Saved Games দিয়ে আপনার Google অ্যাকাউন্টে একটি সেভ রাখে: সবচেয়ে বেশি দূরত্ব ও মোট হিসাব, কড়ি, ঝাড়ু, আপনার কেনা জিনিস আর কখন সেভ হলো। এটা শুধু নতুন ফোনে বা আবার ইনস্টল করার পর অগ্রগতি ফিরিয়ে আনতে কাজে লাগে। এটা Google রাখে, আমরা না; আমাদের কাছে এর কোনো কপি নেই। সেটিংস আর বিজ্ঞাপনের তথ্য এতে থাকে না।

সাইন ইন করা আপনার ইচ্ছা; সাইন ইন ছাড়াও পুরো গেম খেলা যায়। এই গেমের সেভ আর Play Games তথ্য যেকোনো সময় আপনার Play Games প্রোফাইল (play.google.com/games/profile) বা Google অ্যাকাউন্ট থেকে মুছে ফেলতে পারেন।

গেমটি ফ্রি, আর এতে Google LLC-এর Google AdMob দিয়ে বিজ্ঞাপন দেখানো হয়। বিজ্ঞাপন দেখানো, মাপা আর প্রতারণা ঠেকানোর জন্য Google আপনার ফোন থেকে কিছু তথ্য নিতে পারে, যেমন:

  • ফোনের বিজ্ঞাপন আইডি (Android advertising ID),
  • আপনার IP ঠিকানা, যা থেকে আনুমানিক অবস্থান বোঝা যায়,

এই তথ্য Google সংগ্রহ করে, আমরা না, আর তা Google-এর প্রাইভেসি পলিসি মেনে চলে: policies.google.com/privacy।
